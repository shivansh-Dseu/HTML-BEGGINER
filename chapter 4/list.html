Steps to make cake

    1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease the pans.
    2. Cream the softened butter and sugar together until fluffy.
    3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then add vanilla extract.
    4. Alternately add the dry ingredients and milk into the mixture.
    5. Pour into pans and bake for 30 to 35 minutes.

Ingredients

    • All-purpose flour
    • Baking powder
    • Salt
    • Unsalted butter
    • Granulated sugar
    • Eggs
    • Vanilla extract
    • Whole milk
